# Reminders and timers

[All topics](README.md) · [Glossary](glossary.md)

_Ask for a reminder in your own words, anywhere, and see them all on the Reminders page._

Ask your identity to remind you, the way you'd ask a person: "remind me at 7:30 to call mum", "in 20 minutes",
"every weekday at 9".

## Steps

1. Type, text or say it: "Remind me in 2 minutes to stretch."
2. Your identity tells you when it will remind you.
3. At that time the [reminder](glossary.md#reminder) arrives.

You can ask in Chat, from your phone, on Discord, or out loud to a Hologram Companion.

## The Reminders page

1. Open **Reminders**.
2. Under **Add a reminder**, type what and when (like "at 7" or "in 20 minutes").
3. Pick where it reaches you, and press **Add**.

**Next 7 days** lists what is coming, with **Cancel**. Later reminders show 10 at a time with **Show more**, and
missed ones 5 with **Show all**. What you are typing in **Add a reminder** stays when the list changes. Today's
are also on Today under **Coming up**. Cancelling a reminder the Hologram Companion keeps itself removes it from the Hologram Companion too;
if the Hologram Companion is offline, it is removed there when it is back, and the cancel says which.

## Where a reminder reaches you

- **Codie if she is on, else my phone or DM** (the usual choice).
- **Said on the Codie**, **A text to my phone**, **My Discord DM**, or **Only here, in the app**.

## Details

- **A day with a bare hour is asked about.** "Friday at 7" gets "am or pm?" and nothing is set until you say it
  again with one ("friday at 7pm"). "At 12" means noon.
- **"Next Friday" said on a Thursday** could mean tomorrow or the week after, so your identity asks and sets
  nothing. Say "tomorrow at 3pm" or "friday next week at 3pm". On other days "next friday" is the coming Friday.
- Times are this PC's time. "Tomorrow" sent just after midnight means the coming morning.
- A reminder the hub missed because it was off for more than six hours shows under **Missed while the hub was
  off**, not sent late. **Dismiss** clears it.
- Places ("when I get to the office") are not possible yet.
- A real Hologram Companion also has its own alarms: see [Your Hologram Companion](17-your-hologram-companion.md).
