# Glossary

[All topics](README.md)

## Activity

_Also: activity log._

The list of everything the hub did and saw, newest first: messages in and out, approvals, reminders, problems. Open
it from the ☰ menu.

## All details

_Also: Essentials, Essentials and All details, Essentials / All details, More fold, Advanced mode, Advanced, Simple, Simple mode, Simple and Advanced, Simple / Advanced._

How much each page shows. The app starts on Essentials: the everyday cards, with the ones most people never need
in a closed "More" fold at the foot of their page (one click opens it). All details opens every More fold and
shows the technical details. Nothing is out of reach in either. Switch in the ☰ menu or in Settings → Appearance.
(These were called Simple and Advanced.)

## Allowance

_Also: allowances, token approval, Approvals you gave._

A permission you give someone else to take tokens from an identity's wallet later, up to an amount and until a
time. The Wallet lists them under Approvals you gave, each with a Stop button.

## API key

_Also: key from a provider, Anthropic key, OpenAI key._

A secret code from Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT) that lets the app use your own account there. You paste
it once; it stays on this PC. The provider bills you for what you use.

## Approval

_Also: approve, held action, needs your OK._

Something an identity wants to do that waits for your OK first, like sending a message or tokens. You press
Approve or Don't on Today, or answer from your phone.

## Authenticator

_Also: authenticator app, one-time code._

A phone app (such as Google Authenticator or Aegis) that shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds. The hub uses
it so only you can approve a coding task from far away.

## Autonomy

_Also: Propose, Auto, autonomy mode._

How much an identity does on its own when a message comes in. Off: it only acts when you ask. Propose: it
suggests an action and waits for your OK. Auto: it runs the actions you allowed, within limits you set.

## Backup

_Also: back up keys, key backup._

An encrypted file with every identity's key, made with Back up keys in Settings → Security & backups. Keep it somewhere
safe; with it and its passphrase you can bring your identities back on any PC.

## Brain

_Also: brains, AI brain, model._

The AI that writes an identity's replies. Some are free (the on-chain agent, the personas, a local model); Claude
and ChatGPT are paid with your own key.

## Bridge

_Also: bridge token, relay, Waifu AI Bridge._

A small program that runs next to a Hologram Companion in another place and connects out to your hub, so the hub and the Hologram Companion
don't have to share a Wi-Fi.

## Budget

_Also: monthly budget._

The most a paid brain may cost an identity in a month. When it is reached, the identity uses the free on-chain
brain until the 1st and you are told once. A budget of 0 turns paid brains off; no budget means no cap.

## Canister

_Also: canisters, smart contract._

A program that runs on the Internet Computer. The registry and the memory bank are canisters. You never have to
manage one to use the app.

## Claim

_Also: claiming, propose me as owner._

Recording you as the owner of an identity in the registry, signed with your Internet Identity. Optional. It does
not move or copy the key, and it cannot recover a lost key.

## Clean-up

_Also: cleanup, suggested clean-up._

A Memory tab that suggests notes not worth keeping (duplicates, out of date, trivial), each with the reason.
Nothing is forgotten until you tick it and press Forget selected.

## Coding session

_Also: coding sessions, terminal session, coding-session key, agent key._

An AI coding tool running in a terminal on your PC that reports to one of your identities, so you can ask how
it's going from your phone. Each session connects with a coding-session key. Set up under
Settings → Developer → Coding sessions.

## Connector

_Also: ChatGPT connector, ChatGPT memory sharing, memory sharing._

The optional link that lets ChatGPT read the memories you approve from one identity, and ask this hub to send a
message that you then review. Off until you set it up in Memory → ChatGPT memory sharing.

## Context

_Also: recent context._

The last few turns of all your identities' conversations, which every brain reads, so one identity can pick up
where another left off. Shown in Memory → Context.

## Day page

_Also: day pages, week page, week pages, Days._

A few lines about one day, written after you're done for the day from what you and your identities said; on
Sunday night a week page too. "What did we do yesterday?" is answered from them. Shown in Memory → Days.

## Desktop pop-ups

_Also: pop-ups, desktop notifications, notifications._

A note that pops up on this PC when a message comes in, while the page is open. Turn it on in the ☰ menu or in
Settings → Appearance; the browser asks once. Not the same as Quiet & away (what your phone is told) or a Hologram Companion's
speech.

## Developer Portal

_Also: Discord Developer Portal, developer page._

Discord's website for making bots, at discord.com/developers. You make your identity's bot there.

## Direct message

_Also: DM, DMs._

A private chat between you and the bot on Discord, outside any server channel.

## Discord bot token

_Also: bot token, Discord token._

The secret password of a Discord bot, from Bot → Reset Token on the Developer Portal. You paste it into the
Discord card; the hub seals it on this PC and only ever shows its last four characters.

## Discord server

_Also: Discord servers._

A community space on Discord with channels. You need one you own (or may add bots to) before you can invite your
identity's bot. Making one is free.

## Dreaming

_Also: dream, nightly review, Dream now._

Once a day an identity with a Claude or ChatGPT key rereads the day's conversations and tidies the memory: fixes
wrong notes, merges duplicates and keeps a short page per person. Needs a Claude or ChatGPT key.

## Enrol

_Also: enroll, enrolment, enrolled, enrolling._

Letting one of your identities write into your memory bank. One click (Enroll) after you sign in with Internet
Identity; until then what it learns stays on this PC.

## Former name

_Also: former names, old name._

A name an identity had before you renamed it. It still answers to it, and nobody else on the hub can take it,
until you say "forget former names".

## Friend

_Also: friends, friend link._

A contact that gets public answers only (who the identity is, prices, the shop shelf), never your private things.

## Gateway

_Also: connector gateway, trusted gateway._

The web address the ChatGPT connector goes through: a service you run, someone you trust runs for you, or the
official Waifu AI gateway, that stands between this hub and ChatGPT. The hub talks only to a gateway whose address
you typed and trusted. Settings shows who runs it beside its address, in your words.

## Guest

_Also: guests, member, members._

Anyone on your Discord server who isn't you. Guests get chat only, within daily limits you set; being in your
server never makes someone a friend.

## Handoff

_Also: handoff code, conversation handoff._

Carrying a conversation on at another device. You make a code where you are and type it at the other device's
page. The code lasts two minutes and opens once.

## Hologram Companion

_Also: hologram._

A small hologram companion device. Waifu AI gives it a voice, a memory and a way to reach you anywhere. You can
also start with a pretend one (a "mock" Hologram Companion) and connect a real one later.

The app labels your Hologram Companion's identity type "Codie".

## Hub

_Also: the hub, Waifu AI hub._

The Waifu AI program running on your PC. It holds your identities' keys and does all the work; the window you see
is its page.

## Hub export

_Also: export hub, whole hub, Restore hub._

One encrypted file with everything: every identity, settings, contacts, brains, reminders and keys you brought.
It is the file that moves your hub to a new PC. The authenticator key and connected apps' keys stay behind.

## Hub status

_Also: status of the hub._

What your identities know about the hub when you ask in private: whether the dream ran, which coding sessions
are live, whether the Hologram Companion is reachable and how much is waiting for you. Never shown in a server channel.

## ICP

_Also: ICP token._

The token of the Internet Computer. An identity's address can hold it; the app works without any.

## Identity

_Also: identities, AI identity, AI identities._

One AI you run, with its own key on this PC. It is either a Hologram Companion (linked to a hologram device, real or mock) or
an AI identity (no device).

## Intents

_Also: Privileged Gateway Intents, Message Content intent, Message Content._

Switches on Discord's Developer Portal (Bot → Privileged Gateway Intents) that let a bot read more. Leave Message
Content off unless you use listen channels.

## Internet Computer

_Also: IC._

A public network of computers that runs programs called canisters. Each identity's key is an address there; the
registry, Sneed SMS and the memory bank live there.

## Internet Identity

_Also: II, sign in with Internet Identity._

A free sign-in for the Internet Computer, with a passkey on your phone or PC instead of a password. You use it to
claim identities and to manage your memory bank.

## Key

_Also: keys, identity key, private key._

The secret that is an identity. It lives only on this PC. Lose it without a backup and that identity is gone for
good: nothing can recover it.

## LAN tools

_Also: LAN, local network tools._

A setting under Settings → Phone & apps that lets phones and devices on your Wi-Fi reach the hub's key-protected doors. The
app window itself stays private to this PC.

## Local model

_Also: local brain._

A brain that runs on your own PC: free and works offline, but weaker than Claude or ChatGPT.

## Lock

_Also: locked, auto-lock, lock screen._

With a passphrase set, the hub starts locked and can lock itself after a while; nothing works until you type the
passphrase.

## MCP

_Also: outside AI, outside AIs, AI app key, MCP client, Tool API._

A standard way for an AI app on your PC (such as Claude Desktop) to use an identity's tools with a key you give
it (an AI app key). Its card, Outside AIs & devices, is in the More fold of the identity's "Where it lives" tab.

## Memory bank

_Also: bank, memory banks._

An optional shared store on the Internet Computer where all your enrolled identities keep and share what they
remember. Without one, each identity keeps its notes on this PC.

## Mock Hologram Companion

_Also: mock, mock device._

A pretend Hologram Companion: everything works, but spoken replies show as text in the app. Switch it to a real Hologram Companion any time.

## Neuron

_Also: neurons, voting._

Locked tokens that give a vote in an Internet Computer community. An identity can vote with one you share with it,
under Govern on its More tab.

## Notes & facts

_Also: notes, notes and facts, notes on this PC._

The Memory tab that shows what one identity knows: the Hologram Companion's own cards, notes on this PC, the memory bank and the
nightly pages.

## On-chain agent

_Also: Agent, free brain, free on-chain brain._

The free brain that needs no key. It runs on the Internet Computer and can use the identity's tools; it is
simpler than Claude or ChatGPT.

## Orchestrator

_Also: orchestrator role, job, jobs._

One identity you pick to manage the hub for you. In a job you start, it checks on and asks your other identities,
within limits you set on Teamwork. It works under you: it is not a second owner.

## Owner device

_Also: my phone, owner's phone._

A phone or device you told the hub is you. It is answered with full access and can approve things.

## Owner feed

_Also: feed, Report to my phone._

Optional short reports to your phone of what your identities did, every few minutes, with urgent things at once.

## Pairing

_Also: pair, pairing code._

Proving to the hub that a device or account is yours by typing a short code it shows you, once. Used for a real
Hologram Companion, a phone app and your Discord account.

## Passphrase

_Also: passphrases._

A long password that encrypts your keys and private data on this PC. You type it each time the hub starts and
after it locks. Forget it and the keys can't be opened: keep a backup.

## Peer

_Also: peers._

Another of your own identities. Peers talk freely and share your memory bank, but can't approve anything.

## Persona

_Also: personas, Easy, Spicy, Smol, Coder._

A free character brain (Easy, Spicy, Smol, Coder). Personas can't use tools or memory, so they are not offered in
Chat or for answering messages; one can be the Ghostwriter that words a message a brain declined to write.

## Place

_Also: device place, destination._

Your own word for where a device is, such as "Home office". You give a bridge's device a place in Settings →
Memory → Devices and roaming, so you can say where a memory or a reminder should be spoken.

## Principal

_Also: principals, address, ID._

An identity's public address on the Internet Computer, a long string of letters and dashes. Share it so others can
message the identity; it never reveals the key.

## Public companion

_Also: My place, device role._

The role of a Hologram Companion set up for other people, like one in a shop window or at a reception desk: everyone who talks
to it is a guest, you too, and it never says anything meant for you. The other role, My place, is your own Hologram Companion.
Choose it on the identity's "What it may do" tab.

## Public name

_Also: registry name._

An optional name in the registry so others can find and message an identity by name. Public, first come first
served, free; chatting doesn't need one.

## Registry

_Also: directory._

The public list of identity names on the Internet Computer. Contacts → Directory searches it.

## Reminder

_Also: reminders, timer, timers._

Something you asked to be told at a time: "remind me at 7 to call mum", "in 20 minutes". The Reminders page lists
them.

## Roaming

_Also: Devices and roaming, roam._

Using your identities at more than one device, each a Hologram Companion reached through a bridge: recall a memory there, leave
a reminder for that place, or carry a conversation over.

## Saved draft

_Also: draft, drafts, saved drafts._

What you were typing in Chat or Messages, kept on this PC so that a reload does not lose it. You choose to restore
it or discard it; restoring never sends anything.

## Session report

_Also: session reports._

What a coding session may read from the hub when you allow it on its key: Activity, costs, the usage report and the
hub's health. Each row says who, what kind and how it ended. Read-only; never keys, message text or memory.

## Sneed Premium

_Also: Premium._

A Sneed membership that raises an identity's message limits (one message a minute instead of one every ten).
Optional.

## Sneed SMS

_Also: Sneed, Sneed message, Sneed messages._

A text-message service on the Internet Computer. Your identities and your phone can message each other through
it. Messages are not encrypted, and each sender can send one every 10 minutes.

## Standby hub

_Also: standby, Active on this hub, second hub._

A copy of an identity on a second PC that shows messages but answers none, so two hubs never both reply. Switch
it on under This hub, on the identity's More tab, to make that copy the one that acts.

## Stranger

_Also: strangers, unknown sender._

Someone the hub doesn't know. A stranger gets at most a short chat reply with no tools and no memory, or nothing.

## Task approval

_Also: task approvals, coding task._

Approving a job you texted to a coding session, with a code from your authenticator app. Set up in Settings →
Security.

## Telegram

_Also: Telegram bot, BotFather._

A chat app. You can make a free Telegram bot and let one identity answer you through it from your phone.

## Test network

_Also: staging, Sneed staging._

The Sneed SMS network for developers. Messages there reach only other test users. New hubs use the real network;
switch under Settings → Developer.

## Unsafe mode

_Also: unsafe off._

Your choice to drive the hub from your own Discord account with no confirmation. A red banner shows while it is on.
It never lifts the hold on someone else's words, keys always asking, "stop all writes" or doors and the alarm.

## Update channel

_Also: release channel._

Where the hub looks for new versions. Leave it as it is unless you know you want test releases.
