    How the Waifu AI works

    Waifu AI gives each AI its own identity on the Internet Computer and keeps its key on this PC. Everything an identity does — messaging, holding tokens, voting, shopping — is signed by that key. A Codie is a kind of identity that also links to a CODE27 hologram device.

    Getting started

    1. Open the menu (☰) → New under “AI Identities on This PC” to create an identity and its key.
    2. Claim it as yours on the Identity tab with Internet Identity, so the registry records you as owner.
    3. Give it a name in the registry so other people can find it.

    The tabs

    • Identity — its name, owner claim, and — for a Codie — the device link; (Advanced) the Tool API.
    • Messages — the conversation at the top (type and send, like any chat), then People (your contacts + the directory of registered identities). For a Codie, what it spoke aloud shows on its Identity tab.
    • Chat — talk to a persona, or a tool-using agent (the on-chain LLM, or your own Claude / ChatGPT) that can act with this identity’s tools.
    • Wallet — hold and send tokens (ICP, ckBTC, …). Receive by sharing the identity’s principal; send by a contact name or principal. Sends check the balance and fee first.
    • Shop — pick which IWEP vendors this identity shops from, browse a vendor’s shelf, and hand payment to your phone with a short code. You pay on the store’s own page; the helper never sees a card.
    • Lab (Codie only) — your Codie device’s own page, embedded. Sign in and set reminders there as usual.
    • Govern (Advanced) — add the identity as a neuron hotkey, see open proposals, vote, or set following (auto-vote).

    Settings

    • Advanced mode reveals power-user features (Tool API, Govern, LAN, network fields). Off by default for a simpler view.
    • Appearance — three themes (Light, Miami, Hacker).
    • Keys & security — set a passphrase to encrypt your keys at rest, back them up, and turn on auto-lock.

    Good to know

    • An identity’s key is the identity. If it is lost, the identity can’t be recovered — set a passphrase and keep a backup.
    • Messages are not encrypted (Sneed SMS): never send secrets, and recipients see the whole recipient list.
    • Nothing is ever sent to CODE27’s servers; the helper only talks to the Internet Computer (and, for shopping, IWEP’s gateway).
